Third-party licences in sdk-kotlin
==================================

lucide-static (v1.75.0)
-----------------------
The glyph sets mountaya-geo publishes hold the markup of icons from Lucide, printed from the
glyph table of mountaya/schemas into

  mountaya-geo/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/mountaya/geo/generated/GlyphSetMarker.kt
  mountaya-geo/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/mountaya/geo/generated/GlyphSetControl.kt

each carrying the licences below too, as every jar and AAR mountaya-geo publishes does at
META-INF/mountaya-geo/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt: the ISC licence for Lucide, and the MIT
licence for the icons Lucide derives from Feather, which it lists.
